Creative Writing 1

by Angad2005 · English · chat format

A chat-format instruction-tuning dataset focused mainly on creative writing, with smaller portions of code, translation, rewriting and reasoning tasks. Responses are long-form and include a reasoning section before the final answer.

What is inside

  • Format Each line is a JSON object with a messages list (system, user, assistant) and an empty tools field.
  • Reasoning Responses open with a reasoning section (a <think>-style block) before the final answer.
  • Length Mean 3,988 tokens per example; the distribution is bimodal — ~59% under 1,800 tokens, most of the rest between 5,000 and 11,000.
  • Training note 647 examples (40.9%) exceed 4,096 tokens and will truncate at that limit — size your context window accordingly.

Heads-up

Prompts are heavily templated (94% start with “You are an…”) and the mix is skewed toward creative writing. This is a creative-writing SFT set — not a factual knowledge source.
